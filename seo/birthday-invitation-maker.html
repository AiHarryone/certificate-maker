What an Invitation Needs

  • Who's party — name and age if it's a kids' party.
  • Date, time, location — the three things guests need.
  • RSVP line — a phone or email makes it real.
  • Theme words — “superhero”, “unicorn”, “garden party” set the mood.

Make It Get an RSVP

  • Date first — the most-skipped detail; make it bold.
  • One theme color — matches the party, keeps the card clean.
  • Include RSVP contact — cards without it get zero responses.
