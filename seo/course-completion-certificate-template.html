What to Include for Credibility

  • Student name — the credential they show employers.
  • Course title — exactly what they completed.
  • Hours or level — adds credibility.
  • Instructor + date — authority and timing.

Why Certificates Help Courses

  • They convert — students finish courses that offer a certificate.
  • They're shareable — a PNG is easy to add to LinkedIn and resumes.
  • They brand you — a clean certificate makes your course feel more professional than a PDF they could fake.
