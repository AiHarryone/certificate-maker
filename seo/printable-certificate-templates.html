Why Printable Matters

A printable certificate is a physical object — it gets framed, pinned to corkboards, photographed for LinkedIn. The design must hold up on paper: clean borders, balanced layout, enough resolution. Certificate Maker exports print-ready PNGs.

How to Print Like a Pro

  • Print on card stock — 250gsm+ feels premium.
  • Use borderless printing if your template has a full-bleed border.
  • Check the preview — the PNG preview shows exactly what prints.
  • Keep a digital copy — the PNG is also great for email and sharing.
