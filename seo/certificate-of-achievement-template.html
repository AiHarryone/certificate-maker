What to Put on the Certificate

  • Name of the achiever — the centerpiece.
  • The specific achievement — grade, record, result.
  • Date — anchors the moment.
  • Signature or seal — makes it feel official.

Wording Tips

  • Be specific — “for ranking first in the class” beats “for doing great”.
  • Match tone to audience — formal for academic/corporate, warm for kids.
  • Keep it to 2–4 sentences — the certificate is the artifact, not the transcript.
